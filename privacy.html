100% On-Device & Zero Tracking Guarantee

Unplug does not require an account, has no analytics tracking, does not record your screen or camera, and never sends your scroll activity to any remote server.

1. Overview

Unplug Labs ("we", "us", or "our") builds digital well-being tools. This Privacy Policy describes how Unplug ("the App") handles information when you install and use the application on Android devices.

Our core philosophy is simple: your attention and your data belong to you. We do not monetize your data, sell ads, or build user profiles.

2. Use of Android AccessibilityService API

To count the number of short-form videos you watch and help you enforce daily limits, Unplug requires the Android AccessibilityService API.

Here is exactly how Unplug uses this permission:

  • Detection of Reel/Short UI: Unplug monitors the active package name when you open supported apps (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat) to identify when a reel or short video container is on screen.
  • Scroll Gesture Counting: Unplug detects vertical scroll swipe gestures inside these specific short-video containers to increment your daily reel counter.
  • Feed Blocking (Limiter): When you reach your daily user-defined limit, Unplug displays an on-device overlay or navigates back to prevent further scrolling.

What we NEVER do with AccessibilityService:

  • We DO NOT record, inspect, or process your screen content, text, or video stream.
  • We DO NOT log keystrokes, passwords, private messages, or personal chats.
  • We DO NOT access your microphone, camera, or personal media files.
  • Accessibility events are processed strictly in RAM in real-time and are never stored or transmitted.

3. Data Storage & Retention

All data generated by Unplug—including your daily reel count, limit thresholds, and battle statistics—is stored in an encrypted local database (SQLite) directly on your device.

If you uninstall the app or clear app data in Android Settings, all counters and history are permanently deleted from your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

The free version of Unplug functions 100% offline without requiring an internet connection. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK).

If you purchase an optional Unplug Plus subscription through Google Play In-App Billing or UPI, transactions are processed directly through Google Play or certified payment gateways according to their respective privacy terms.

5. Children's Privacy

Unplug does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages seeking to reduce screen addiction.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
