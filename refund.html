7-Day No-Questions-Asked Refund Guarantee

If Unplug Plus doesn't help you cut down your reel time or you are not completely satisfied, simply email us within 7 days of your purchase for a full 100% refund.

1. Free vs. Paid Features

The core functionality of Unplug (live reel counter, 5-state brain progress meter, and 1v1 battles with friends) is 100% free forever. You do not need to pay anything to take control of your screen time.

Unplug Plus is an optional upgrade offering automated hard feed blockers, break timers, and advanced custom schedules.

2. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund under the following conditions:

  • First-Time Purchases: You request a refund within 7 days of your initial subscription purchase (annual or monthly plan).
  • Technical Incompatibility: In the rare event that Unplug cannot detect reel scroll gestures on your specific Android phone model or OS version, and our engineering team cannot resolve it within 48 hours, you are entitled to a full refund regardless of plan duration.
  • Accidental Renewals: If your annual subscription renewed automatically and you contact us within 48 hours of the charge, we will refund the renewal charge in full.

3. How to Request a Refund

You can request a refund using any of the following methods:

  • Via Direct Email: Send an email to hello@unplugapp.ai with the subject line Refund Request — [Your Order ID or UPI ID]. We process email refunds within 24–48 business hours.
  • Via Google Play: If purchased through Google Play, visit Google Play Order History, locate the Unplug Plus transaction, and click "Request a refund".

4. Cancellation Terms

You can cancel your subscription at any time with a single tap. There are no cancellation fees or retention traps:

  • Open the Google Play Store app on your phone.
  • Tap your profile icon ➔ Payments & subscriptions ➔ Subscriptions.
  • Select Unplug Plus and tap Cancel subscription.

After cancellation, you will retain access to Plus features until the conclusion of your current paid billing period.
