How do I see how many reels I watch?

Install Unplug and turn on its Accessibility permission. It counts every reel and short you scroll and shows the number live on your screen.

Which apps does Unplug work with?

Instagram and Instagram Lite, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and Facebook Lite, and Snapchat.

Can Unplug block Reels and Shorts?

Yes. With Unplug Plus you set a daily reel limit. When you reach it, Unplug blocks Reels and Shorts inside the app while the rest of the app keeps working.
